Reproducible Science Audits
This appendix audits 3 preregistered studies conducted with the Reproducible Science
toolkit: two under journal review and one in progress. Each audit gives a
description of the study, an example finding, what each command found in its repository,
and the findings made while it was written. Table gives the result from
a clone for the two under review, and Table their findings.
What the toolkit established from a clone of each project, at the pinned commit.

Findings are coded from each project's records: commits, ledger events, plan logs, claim files and
session transcripts. A finding counts
when a command, a script that performed the same check before the command existed, a hook, or an act
the toolkit requires (sealing an input, binding a number, pinning a
quotation) exposed it and a record says so. The coding rules were fixed before the records were read.
Defects found by a person or raised in review are not counted as findings.
Findings, by project and severity. A claim finding would change a
reported number or conclusion. A source finding is a value or reference attributed to a work
that does not contain it. A record finding is a break in provenance that leaves every reported number
correct.

Audit: Cross-design evidence discordance
Description. A preregistered study of drug-trial outcomes. https://github.com/elliottower/cross-design-evidence-discordance, commit c6a7a06.
The clone carries 365 tracked files, 6 registration documents, 94 claim records, 175 quotations and 75 bibliography entries.
All Findings. 31: 5 claim, 14 source, 8 record, 4 cosmetic.
Example Finding. results verify found two recorded files edited since they were recorded and one that had moved.
Reviews. Of the 31 findings: 0 before submission, 11 review round 1, 2 review round 2, 0 review round 3, 17 review round 4, 1 after round 4.

Audit: Knockout epistasis
Description. A study of knockout epistasis in Boolean gene regulatory networks, also covered in Section. https://github.com/elliottower/knockout-epistasis-dynamics, commit cb82384.
The clone carries 1,267 tracked files, 3 registered plans, 153 ledger events, 13 runs, 131 manuscript claims bound to a run, 7 claim records and 18 quotations.
All Findings. 18: 3 claim, 2 source, 10 record, 3 cosmetic.
Example Finding. Binding the manuscript's numbers to runs required recomputing them, and the recomputation revealed that a property stated for all 28 networks holds only for 27.
